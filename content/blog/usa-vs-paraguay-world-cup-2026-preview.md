## The Match

**USA vs Paraguay**
Group D · Match 1 for both teams
**Date:** Friday, June 12, 2026
**Kickoff:** 9:00 PM ET / 6:00 PM PT
**Venue:** SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, California
**Capacity:** ~70,000

This is the opening match for the United States in their home World Cup — and it's at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, one of the most spectacular venues in all of American sports. The USMNT has waited for this moment. So have 70,000 fans in Inglewood.

## What's at Stake

The 2026 World Cup is on American soil — co-hosted by the USA, Canada, and Mexico — and the host nation is desperate to make a statement. The USMNT's run to the Round of 16 in Qatar 2022 was genuine progress, but expectations are higher now. The squad is older, more experienced, and playing at home.

Paraguay are a team that makes life difficult — organized defensively, dangerous on the counter, and with nothing to lose as an underdog. They're the kind of opponent that can derail an opening match if the home side is nervous or disorganized.

**Group D:** USA, Paraguay, Australia, Türkiye

The USA are heavy favorites to advance from this group. But opener results matter enormously for momentum, qualification math, and the energy of an entire nation.

## Group D Breakdown

**USA:** The hosts. Carrying enormous expectation. Blend of MLS and European-based players with experience from Qatar 2022. The atmosphere of a home World Cup, particularly at SoFi Stadium, gives them an intangible advantage that's hard to quantify.

**Paraguay:** Qualified through CONMEBOL, which is always a legitimate credential. Solid defensive organization, difficult to break down. Not at their historic peak but dangerous enough to take a point from anyone on the right day.

**Australia:** The Socceroos have punched above their weight in recent tournaments. Qualifying from Asia despite a difficult group. They play USA in Seattle on June 19 — if USA slip against Paraguay, that match becomes must-win.

**Türkiye:** A strong European side with technical quality and tactical discipline. Türkiye vs Paraguay (June 19, San Francisco) is the group's most balanced fixture. Türkiye plays the USA in the final group game in Los Angeles.

**Realistic USA scenario:** Win this opener, then navigate Australia and Türkiye to advance as group winners or runners-up. A draw against Paraguay would be recoverable but creates unnecessary pressure.

## The Los Angeles Experience

SoFi Stadium is in Inglewood, 15 miles from downtown Los Angeles. **Do not drive.** The stadium has limited parking and the I-405 is a parking lot on match nights.

**Best transit options:**
- **Metro C Line (Green Line):** Connects to the Crenshaw/LAX Transit Project and has a dedicated sports shuttle connection to SoFi. Check Metro schedules closer to match day.
- **Rideshare:** Uber/Lyft drop-offs are designated — follow official SoFi Stadium guidance for pick-up zones post-match.
- **Official shuttles:** SoFi Stadium operates shuttles from designated park-and-ride locations throughout LA County.

**Pre-match in Inglewood:**
- **YouTube Theater forecourt** — The plaza area around SoFi Stadium hosts pre-match entertainment for major events
- **The Hangar** (Inglewood) — The best nearby option for a pre-match drink without going back into LA proper

**Pre-match in LA proper:**
The best soccer bars in Los Angeles for watching or gathering before the match include **Continental Club** (Los Feliz), **The Pikey** (Hollywood), and **Ye Olde King's Head** (Santa Monica). Get there early — a Friday evening USA opener in LA will see every soccer bar in the city at capacity.

## Where to Watch Without Tickets

If you don't have a ticket, Los Angeles will host massive official and unofficial watch parties. Look for:

- **Grand Park** (Downtown LA) — The city's main public gathering space typically activates for major USA matches with big screens
- **LA Live** (Downtown) — The entertainment complex adjacent to Crypto.com Arena typically sets up official viewing areas for major events
- **Soccer-specific bars** — Continental Club, The Brig (Venice), The Pikey, Ye Olde King's Head. Expect standing room 2+ hours before kickoff.

## Prediction

Paraguay will be well-organized and competitive in the first half. The sheer volume of a home crowd at SoFi Stadium — and the quality advantage the USA possesses — should tell in the second half.

**USA 2–0 Paraguay.** A clean sheet matters for goal difference if Group D tightens.
