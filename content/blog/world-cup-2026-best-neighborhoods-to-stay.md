## How to Pick Where to Stay

The 2026 World Cup is spread across 16 cities in three countries. Stadium locations vary wildly — some are downtown, some are suburban, some require significant transit planning. Getting your neighborhood right determines whether match days feel effortless or exhausting.

This guide covers the optimal neighborhoods in each host city, ranked by the combination of factors that matter most: transit to the stadium, access to bars and restaurants, and value for money.

---

## USA Host Cities

### New York / New Jersey
**MetLife Stadium is in East Rutherford, NJ — about 8 miles from Midtown Manhattan.**

🥇 **Midtown Manhattan** — The obvious choice. Penn Station provides direct NJ Transit to the stadium. Everything in New York is accessible. Hotels are expensive but you're in New York.

🥈 **Jersey City / Hoboken** — 20–30% cheaper than Manhattan, 10 minutes from Penn Station by PATH train, and increasingly vibrant bar scenes. A great option for longer stays.

🥉 **East Rutherford / Secaucus** — Budget option right next to the stadium. Minimal nightlife but maximally convenient for match days. Marriott Meadowlands is the classic choice.

---

### Los Angeles
**SoFi Stadium is in Inglewood — 15 miles from downtown LA.**

🥇 **West Hollywood / Hollywood** — Best proximity to bar scene and has reasonably viable Metro/shuttle access to SoFi. Hotel options: Andaz West Hollywood, 1 Hotel West Hollywood.

🥈 **Downtown LA** — Connected to the Metro C Line shuttle system for SoFi. Close to LA Live fan zone. More affordable than WeHo.

🥉 **Inglewood** — Right next to the stadium. Limited nightlife but budget-friendly and maximally convenient for match days.

---

### Miami
**Hard Rock Stadium is in Miami Gardens — 17 miles from South Beach.**

🥇 **Brickell / Downtown Miami** — Best transit options, closest to Brightline and county bus connections. Modern hotels, excellent restaurants.

🥈 **South Beach** — The experience choice. Beautiful, vibrant, and with great bars — but the furthest from the stadium. Use Uber or dedicated shuttles.

🥉 **Miami Gardens** — Stadium-adjacent suburban option. No nightlife but genuinely convenient for match days. Courtyard by Marriott Miami Gardens is the standard choice.

---

### Dallas
**AT&T Stadium is in Arlington — 20 miles from downtown Dallas.**

🥇 **Uptown Dallas** — Best bar scene, good rideshare access to Arlington, central location. Hotels: Hotel ZaZa, The Adolphus.

🥈 **Deep Ellum** — More alternative, excellent restaurants, direct rideshare to the stadium. Boutique hotel options.

🥉 **Arlington** — Stadium-adjacent. Limited walkable entertainment but functional for match-focused trips. Live! by Loews Arlington is adjacent to the stadium.

---

### San Francisco / Bay Area
**Levi's Stadium is in Santa Clara — 45 miles from San Francisco.**

🥇 **San Francisco Downtown/SoMa** — The city experience. Caltrain to Santa Clara in 55 minutes. Worth the fare for the city itself.

🥈 **San Jose Downtown** — 10 minutes from the stadium. Much cheaper than SF. Good restaurant scene. Best value option in the Bay Area.

🥉 **Santa Clara / Sunnyvale** — Walkable/short shuttle to stadium. No real city experience but maximally convenient. Good if you're attending multiple Bay Area matches.

---

### Seattle
**Lumen Field is downtown Seattle — walkable from most central neighborhoods.**

🥇 **Downtown / Pioneer Square** — 10-minute walk to Lumen Field. In the heart of everything. Hotels: Grand Hyatt Seattle, The Edgewater.

🥈 **Capitol Hill** — 20-minute walk or 10-minute bus to the stadium. Best bar and restaurant scene in Seattle. More neighborhood character.

🥉 **SoDo** — Adjacent to the stadium. Industrial neighborhood but extremely convenient. Boutique hotel options limited but proximity unbeatable.

---

### Boston
**Gillette Stadium is in Foxborough — 28 miles south of Boston.**

🥇 **Downtown Boston / Back Bay** — 10-minute walk to South Station for the commuter rail to Foxborough. The full Boston experience. Hotels: Omni Boston Seaport, Fairmont Copley Plaza.

🥈 **Cambridge** — Slightly cheaper, great bars and restaurants, 15 minutes to South Station by Red Line. Best overall value in the Boston area.

🥉 **Foxborough area** — For fans attending multiple Gillette matches. Cheaper, closer to the stadium, but requires a rental car. Limited nightlife.

---

### Philadelphia
**Lincoln Financial Field is in South Philadelphia — 6 miles from Center City.**

🥇 **Center City (Market Street corridor)** — 30-minute subway to the stadium. Everything walkable. Best hotel concentration. Hotels: The Kimpton Hotel Monaco, The Logan.

🥈 **Fishtown** — Hippest neighborhood, 20-minute transit to stadium, excellent bar scene. Boutique hotels and Airbnb options.

🥉 **South Philadelphia** — Closest to the stadium, authentic Philly neighborhood, best cheesesteaks. Limited hotels but excellent Airbnb availability.

---

### Kansas City
**Arrowhead Stadium is 10 miles east of downtown.**

🥇 **Westport / Country Club Plaza** — Best bar scene, most hotel options, reasonable rideshare to Arrowhead. Hotels: InterContinental Kansas City, The Raphael Hotel.

🥈 **Power & Light District (Downtown)** — Fan zone central, connected to official match-day shuttles. Good for fans prioritizing the World Cup experience over neighborhood character.

🥉 **Near the Sports Complex** — Hotels in the Truman Sports Complex area for maximum stadium convenience. Minimal nightlife.

---

### Atlanta
**Mercedes-Benz Stadium is downtown Atlanta — served by MARTA.**

🥇 **Midtown Atlanta** — Best bar scene, MARTA Gold/Red line direct to the stadium, excellent hotels. Hotels: W Atlanta Midtown, Loews Atlanta Hotel.

🥈 **Downtown/Vine City** — Adjacent to the stadium. Walking distance for match days, connected to everything by MARTA. More affordable.

🥉 **Buckhead** — Atlanta's upscale neighborhood, 20 minutes by MARTA. Good for visitors who want luxury accommodation with transit access to matches.

---

### Houston
**NRG Stadium is in the Medical Center — served by METRO Purple Line.**

🥇 **Midtown Houston** — Best soccer bar scene, one METRO stop from NRG Stadium, good hotel options. Hotels: Marriott Houston Medical Center, Hilton Americas.

🥈 **Downtown Houston** — Connected to NRG by METRO, close to all amenities. The Houstonian, Marriott Marquis Houston.

🥉 **Medical Center / NRG area** — Stadium-adjacent, functional, limited nightlife. Useful for arriving the day of a match and leaving the next morning.

---

## Canada Host Cities

### Toronto
**BMO Field is on the waterfront — streetcar or GO Train from downtown.**

🥇 **King West / Entertainment District** — 20-minute walk or short streetcar to BMO Field. Best nightlife. Hotels: Bisha Hotel, The Anndore House.

🥈 **Harbourfront** — Closest neighborhood to the stadium. Beautiful waterfront setting. Hotels: Hotel X Toronto (literally adjacent to Exhibition Place).

🥉 **Yorkville** — Toronto's upscale neighborhood. Further from the stadium but world-class hotels and restaurants. Four Seasons Toronto, Park Hyatt.

---

### Vancouver
**BC Place is downtown Vancouver — walkable from most central areas.**

🥇 **Yaletown** — Walking distance to BC Place, excellent restaurants and bars. Hotels: Opus Vancouver, JW Marriott Parq (attached to BC Place).

🥈 **Gastown** — 20-minute walk to stadium, most atmospheric neighborhood in Vancouver. Good Airbnb and boutique hotel options.

🥉 **West End / Coal Harbour** — Walkable, close to Stanley Park, slightly more affordable. Hotels: Westin Bayshore, Pinnacle Hotel Harbourfront.

---

## Mexico Host Cities

### Mexico City
**Estadio Azteca is in southern Mexico City — Metro Line 2 or 12.**

🥇 **Polanco / Condesa / Roma** — The best neighborhoods in Mexico City. Vibrant restaurant and bar scenes, good hotel options, Metro access to the Azteca. Hotels: W Mexico City, St. Regis Mexico City, Hôtel Carlota.

🥈 **Centro Histórico** — Historic center, budget-friendly, direct Metro to the stadium. Cultural experience unlike any other host city neighborhood.

🥉 **Coyoacán** — Bohemian neighborhood near the stadium (15–20 minutes). More residential, excellent cafes and markets.

---

### Guadalajara
**Estadio Akron is in Zapopan — 20 minutes from downtown Guadalajara.**

🥇 **Chapultepec / Providencia** — Best bar scene in Guadalajara, modern hotels, Uber to the stadium in 20–30 minutes.

🥈 **Historic Center** — Budget-friendly, beautiful colonial architecture, direct buses to the stadium.

---

### Monterrey
**Estadio BBVA is in San Nicolás — 20 minutes from downtown.**

🥇 **Barrio Antiguo** — Monterrey's bar and arts district. The best nightlife in the city. Hotels: Quinta Real Monterrey, Fiesta Americana.

🥈 **San Pedro Garza García** — Upscale Monterrey suburb with excellent hotels and restaurants.

---

## The Bottom Line

**Best overall match-day experience:** Seattle (Lumen Field is downtown, walkable, transit everywhere) and Philadelphia (stadium accessible, city is compact, historic character).

**Most exciting city to stay in:** Mexico City (Polanco/Condesa), New York (anywhere), and Toronto (King West).

**Best value for fans on a budget:** Kansas City (Westport area), Philadelphia (Fishtown), and Vancouver (Gastown with Airbnb).

**Most overpriced for what you get:** Miami Beach (beautiful but stadium is miles away), San Francisco during the tournament (premium prices for suburb stadium access).
